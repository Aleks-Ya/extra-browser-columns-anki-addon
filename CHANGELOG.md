# Changelog for "Extra Browser Columns" Anki addon

## [v1.0.3](https://github.com/Aleks-Ya/extra-browser-columns-anki-addon/releases/tag/v1.0.3) - 2026-11-22
1. __[BUGFIX]__ Double-click on a column's header border shrank the column only to 50 px instead of its `✓`
1. __[FEATURE]__ "Marked" column is the first of the addon's columns in the column list

## [v1.0.2](https://github.com/Aleks-Ya/extra-browser-columns-anki-addon/releases/tag/v1.0.2) - 2026-11-22
1. __[FEATURE]__ Double-click on a column's header border shrinks the column to its `✓` (the header shows the beginning of the name)
1. __[FEATURE]__ Hovering a column header shows the column name and description

## [v1.0.1](https://github.com/Aleks-Ya/extra-browser-columns-anki-addon/releases/tag/v1.0.1) - 2026-11-21
1. __[FEATURE]__ Sortable "New", "Learning" and "Review" columns in the Browser

## [v1.0.0](https://github.com/Aleks-Ya/extra-browser-columns-anki-addon/releases/tag/v1.0.0) - 2026-11-21
1. __[FEATURE]__ Sortable "Suspended", "Buried" and "Marked" columns in the Browser
